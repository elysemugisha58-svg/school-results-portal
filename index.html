Platform

Built for real schools, not demo schools.

Strict multi-tenant isolation

School A can never read or write School B's records. Every document lives under a schoolId scope enforced by security rules.

Automatic academic engine

Enter subject marks once — totals, subject averages, overall term averages and class rankings calculate themselves.

Email sign-in for every role

Admins, teachers, parents and students all sign in with email and password — no SMS costs, no phone verification friction.

CPD & attendance tracking

Schedule workshops and staff meetings, let teachers check in, and generate participation reports over time.

Fee-gated parent results

Parents see their child's marks only after the school confirms fees are cleared — enforced both in the interface and the database rules.

Frugal on bandwidth

Mobile-first layouts, system fonts, and a one-tap low-power mode that drops 3D graphics on older smartphones.

How it works

Live in three steps.

  1. 01

    Register your school

    Submit your school profile. The platform administrator approves it and creates your isolated workspace.

  2. 02

    Set up classes & staff

    Add academic years and terms, create classes and subjects, register teachers and students, and assign teaching loads.

  3. 03

    Start recording

    Teachers enter marks and attendance, parents and students see results instantly, admins watch the reports build themselves.
